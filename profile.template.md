# Candidate profile - REPLACE THIS WITH YOUR OWN

This file is fed to Claude verbatim as the matching baseline. Everything below is
a placeholder — rewrite all of it before your first run.

**Keep it honest.** The "honest gaps" section is what stops Claude handing out
85s for roles you would not clear a screen for. A profile that only lists
strengths produces a report full of false positives, which is worse than no
report at all.

## Basics
- Location: <city, state> (which metros you target; note if open to remote)
- Work authorization: <citizen / permanent resident / needs sponsorship>.
  If you need sponsorship, say so — the scoring uses it to drop postings that
  require citizenship or a security clearance.
- Seeking: <full-time entry level / mid-level / internship>

## Education
- <Degree, institution, dates, location>
- <Relevant coursework>
- <Certifications, test scores>

## Demonstrated skills (things you actually built, not just listed)
- <Tool/language>: what you built with it, at what scale. Numbers help —
  "500+ trading days of data", "33-stock universe", not "experience with Python".
- <Domain skill>: the specific methods you applied.
- <Work experience>: what you owned, what came out of it.

## Honest gaps — weigh these down, do not hide them
- <Skills the job market wants that you do not have yet>
- <Experience types you lack — production systems, US work history, a degree
  in a specific field>
- <Role families that are out of reach and should be ignored>

## What a strong match looks like
<Two or three sentences describing the roles you actually want, the seniority,
and the kind of employer. Be specific — this is what Claude ranks against.>
